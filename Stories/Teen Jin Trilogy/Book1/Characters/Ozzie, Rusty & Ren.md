Ossie, Rusty & Ren are an antagonistic Trio in the so-called *Teen-Jin-Trilogy.* They are fellow school mates of [Jin's'](</Stories/Teen Jin Trilogy/Book1/Characters/Jin Naidesh I.md>) at **Enjar MID School** in [Haarafir](</Lygea/Völker/Bardeena/Locations/Haarafir.md>), loosely base on the [Terrible Trio]( https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/Main/TerribleTrio) Trope.

# General
They might not be the worst students, but they have the reputation of being troublemakers that like to tease or bully their fellow classmates. What they exceed at is the  subject of Elemental Mysticism. They are top of school, together with [Jin](</Stories/Teen Jin Trilogy/Book1/Characters/Jin Naidesh I.md>), [Sora](</Stories/Teen Jin Trilogy/Book1/Characters/Sora Exion I.md>), and a handful of others.
# Vysin "Rusty" Duran

![Foo|272](https://i.pinimg.com/736x/c6/16/98/c6169821669bfa084bbadf1afdb71d23.jpg)

Vysin, mostly called Rusty, is the leader of the trio. She's irascible, quick to anger, and doesn't take shit from anyone. She likes to make fun of others and quickly turns to violence if that doesn't work.

- **Species**: Hybrid - Dragnarian (dominant) - Teyan/Forasian
- **Element**: Metal -> Water
- **Skills**: Strong, Rust Skin
# Renate "Ren" Sinoca

![Renate|258](https://i.pinimg.com/736x/0b/55/2e/0b552e2e4894abb4d47ff5257c4395f0.jpg)

Ren is a scheming, brainy girl, with a very cruel sense of humor. She stays cool in most situations, to the degree of being cold and unfeeling. She's often the one providing the team with ideas on who to bully next. The fact that she lost her voice does not mellow her tendencies.

- **Species**: Hybrid - Rowagian (dominant) - Dragnarian
- **Element**: Air -> Ice
- **Skills**: Quick & Dexterous, Precise High Pressure Cold Shots
- **Notes**: Snips her fingers, if she has something to "say"
## Ossner "Ozzie" Barechna

![Ossner|325](https://i.pinimg.com/736x/0c/ac/d9/0cacd98b1730f766ec707fa07b5c4cec.jpg)

Ozzie is the (only) heart of the trio. He is cheery with a not to threatening disposition. His clumsiness is usually the reason why the team's schemes fail, but his cuteness always appeases the anger of the other two. They just can't stay mad at him.

- **Species**: Hybrid - Nerivan (dominant) - Teyan
- **Element**: Light -> Crystal
- **Skills**: Slow & Clumsy, Golden Power Star Projectiles (which heal on impact)
- **Notes**: His teammates' forgiveness is a miracle in and of itself, since he's convinced of his Power Stars being an actualy attack.
