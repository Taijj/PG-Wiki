# Physical
Even though he's a rather short [Teyan](</Lygea/Völker/Die Teyaner/Die Teyaner.md>), he seems tall compared to other [Bardeenians](</Lygea/Völker/Bardeena/Bardeena.md>). He keeps his hair short and rather messy. Otherwise his looks are rather general.
He wears the typical Uniform of his rank in the Haarafir Guard, a mixture of medival forest ranger and modern soldier, policeman, or scout. The green cape marks his experience beyond the city walls.

He usually keeps his main arms locked behind his back until things get serious. He does most things with his left side arm, which he claims to be his "strong" arm. He got a Tattoo over his mouth consisting of two straight lines. However, he is completely ignorant of their [traditional meaning](</Lygea/Völker/Die Teyaner/Die Teyaner.md#Lügen- und Schweigetattoo>).
# Personality
James is a curious, eager, and thorough investigator. He managed to built a mixed reputation inside the Guard: On the one hand, he is known for his sharp mind, hard working attitude and general alertness. On the other, his wild theories and paranoia are subject of secret ridicule and mockery.
He himself does not care either way. He just keeps focus on his current objective, assigned or self-determined. While keeping an open mind for any possibility, no matter how far fetched.
# Personal
James lives to work. He loves his guarding duties and fulfills them with sometimes unhealthy diligence. As far as anybody knows, he doesn't seem to have any relations inside the country or out. He prefers to keep to himself.
# Social
With friends it is with family. He doesn't seem to have any. Its generally rare to spot him inside the city walls, were he always seems out of place. He likes to spent most of his time in the wilderness or inside the wall building. It is assumed that is were he sleeps, too, since no official address is known to anyone.
# Professional
He's a five star Tracker in the Haarafir Guard, which is the highest rank in that field. He knows the wilderness like the back of his hand, and has lead many an expedition to a successful end. However, he is rather lost inside the city walls, and gets confused quickly by all the noise.
He's a very capable [Earth-Mystic](</Lygea/Das Universum/Mystik/Die Mystik.md>) and possesses the heightened [touch senses of the Teyans](</Lygea/Völker/Die Teyaner/Die Teyaner.md#Physik>). He's an excellent Tracker and has a broad knowledge of the local Anomi and their movements.
# Additional Notes
The [Department of War](Lygea/Völker/Bardeena/State/The%20Bardeena%20Defense%20Force.md#Department of War) commissioned him personally as a kind of watchdog. He is the one who is supposed to keep a close eye on the movements and activities of [Nia](</Stories/Teen Jin Trilogy/Book1/Characters/Aunt Nia.md>), especially while she's outside the city walls. James tries to rise up to the challenge. It excites him deeply, since his target is more than a match and often eludes him.
Outwardly he is very suspicious and critical of her. He often provokes her into verbal, and sometimes physical, fights. However, secretly a deep and immense admiration for the woman has grown inside James. His constant presence in turn also affects Nia. First a constant source of annoyance, quickly turned into a source of amusement. His tenacity, however, leaves its marks and there's definite [phyiscal sexual tension](https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/Main/SlapSlapKiss) growing on here.
