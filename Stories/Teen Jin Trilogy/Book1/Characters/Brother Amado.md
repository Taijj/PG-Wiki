# Physical
Brother Amado is a very old [Dragnarian](</Lygea/Völker/Die Dragnarier/Die Dragnarier.md>) man with a short haircut and a short tail. His wrinkly skin distinctly shows the marks of age, as well as a loose spotted pattern. His age clearly shows in the slow way he moves. His back is always bend under the weight of all of his years, and his eyes mostly droop to low slits. 
He usually wears reserved and simple clothes, a pair of rounded glasses and his walking cane.
# Personality
Despite the toil of age, he always wears a warm smile on his face. He speaks in a calm and quite voice, in short sentences and usually is met with a bag of consumables, which he hands out to the congregation's children.
He is very devout in his faith and thanks the [Goddess](</Stories/Teen Jin Trilogy/Book1/More/The Nethiel Church.md#The Light>) for every day, taking the good and the bad with a patient and enduring humility.
# Personal
He lives alone in a small apartment in [Haarafir](</Lygea/Völker/Bardeena/Locations/Haarafir.md>), managing everyday live with the patience of a saint.
# Social
He will not go out of his way to ask, but gladly accepts help from his fellow [Nethiel Sisters](</Stories/Teen Jin Trilogy/Book1/More/The Nethiel Church.md>). Especially by [Mayra](</Stories/Teen Jin Trilogy/Book1/Characters/Mayra Naidesh.md>) and [Debrann Naidesh](</Stories/Teen Jin Trilogy/Book1/Characters/Debrann Naidesh.md>), who go out of their way, to take him to church, and otherwise take care of his needs.
# Professional
Brother Amado lives retired. Of his former occupations, nothing is known.
